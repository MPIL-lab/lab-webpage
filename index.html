The Multimodal Perception and Intelligence Learning Lab studies embodied intelligence, human behavior modeling and understanding, digital health, multimodal large foundation models, and trustworthy AI. We develop reliable intelligent systems that integrate multimodal perception, learning, and reasoning to understand people, interact with physical environments, and operate safely in real-world settings.
Our lab is currently looking for motivated PhD Students and Visiting Students interested in multimodal AI, embodied intelligence, computer vision, and digital health. If interested, kindly contact us via email: j.liu81@lancaster.ac.uk.
